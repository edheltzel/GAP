# SCOUT / read-only missions

Investigation is not authorization to change code.

## Rules

- Scout / diagnose / plan tasks deliver a report, not a PR.
- Stay read-only unless the ask clearly promotes to ship.
- Do not "fix forward" from a diagnostic finding without a new go-ahead.
- Cite paths, commands, and evidence; do not invent behavior.

## Done

A scout ends with findings, gaps, and recommended next ask - no silent mutations.
