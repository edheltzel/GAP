# Canonical Definitions

## Shipping
Launching something — getting it live, in front of users. Not just committing code, but actually deploying and making it available. Shipping beats perfection.

## Alpha
A unique insight, edge, or advantage that others don't have. Information asymmetry that creates opportunity. Not just "good info" — specifically information that gives you an edge.

## Leverage
A force multiplier — doing more with less. Systems, tools, automation, delegation, or positioning that amplifies output disproportionate to input. Time leverage > money leverage.

## Second Brain
An external system for capturing, organizing, and retrieving knowledge. Based on Tiago Forte's PARA method (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives) combined with Forever Notes (adapted as "Field Notes") and bullet journaling principles. The goal: never lose an idea, always find what you need.

## PARA
Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives — Tiago Forte's organizational framework for a second brain. Projects = active with deadlines. Areas = ongoing responsibilities. Resources = topics of interest. Archives = completed or inactive items.

## Field Notes
Ed's adaptation of Forever Notes — persistent, evolving notes that capture knowledge across time. Combined with PARA and bullet journaling for a hybrid note-taking system.

## Generalist
Someone who cultivates breadth across many domains rather than extreme depth in one. A strength, not a weakness. "Jack of all trades, master of none, is still better than a master of one." Cross-domain thinking produces unique solutions specialists miss.

## Functional Print
A 3D print designed to solve a real problem — an enclosure, a tool, a part, a fixture. Not a fidget, toy, or decoration. The value is in the problem it solves, not the object itself.

## Stack
The complete set of technologies used in a project — languages, frameworks, databases, hosting, CI/CD, and tooling. Also used informally to mean "the full picture" of any system.

## DX
Developer Experience — how pleasant and productive it is to work with a tool, framework, or codebase. Good DX = less friction, faster shipping.

## Ship It
Decision to launch despite imperfection. Done is better than perfect. Iterate after shipping, not before.

## Playbook
A structured approach or set of guidelines for how to handle a specific situation. Not rigid rules — a framework that gets interpreted and adapted to context.
