# Beliefs

## Faith
- God is Lord and controls all. This is the foundation for my family and me.

## How to Treat People
- Treat people the way you want to be treated, as stated in the Bible.
- Even if it means sacrificing some things for yourself and your own personal well-being.
- Helping others is a true calling — wanting to help where people need help.

## Technology as Service
- Technology can be a vehicle for helping people.
- This is why I program and build — especially around AI tools and medical equipment.

## Work and Building
- Build for the client but design for reuse — double duty on the same dollars and the same time.
- Don't reinvent the wheel. Stay DRY in business, not just in code.
- Whatever I build for a customer, I think through what I can use to expand my internal systems.
