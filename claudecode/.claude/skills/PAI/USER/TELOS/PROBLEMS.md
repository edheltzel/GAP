## PeptideWare — Operational Consistency (April 2026, reframed from March "Sales Decline")
Not a demand problem — a **variance** problem. Averages ~$3K/month but inconsistently; single operational events (lost shipments, chargebacks) can wipe a meaningful share of monthly revenue. Goal: **1 order/day consistently** would mitigate most non-MyChron financial concerns.

**Constraints now understood:**
- High-risk merchant classification --> card descriptor and chargeback mechanics are federally regulated and not fixable at the merchant level.
- LinkMoney (bank-to-bank) has ~12% fallback-to-CC rate; fees are lowest available and tolerated in exchange.
- New suppliers found --> previous single-supplier / single-carrier risk is resolved.
- "Duplicate orders" are Ed's deliberate customer-service choice (reship-on-issue), not a broken operational pattern.

Target: stabilize the floor, then think about demand growth.
