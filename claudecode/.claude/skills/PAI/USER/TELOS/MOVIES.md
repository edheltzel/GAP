# Movies

- *Forrest Gump* — A masterpiece showing how a simple-minded individual can live a fantastic life by just doing the work, going through the motions, taking opportunity when it arises, not overthinking, and just doing.
- *The NeverEnding Story* — Inspirational for the creative side of thinking. How imagination reflects in approach to the world.
- *BeastMaster* (1982) — A formative 80s film about doing the right thing no matter what.
- *Star Wars* (Original Trilogy) — Influential in how Ed thinks. The hero's journey and moral clarity.
- *Star Trek: The Next Generation* — Direct inspiration for Atlas and the voice server. The ship's computer you could talk to anywhere on the bridge is what Ed built.
- *Casino* — Big personalities and power dynamics. Reading the room.
- *Reservoir Dogs* — Reading the room. Understanding people. Conservative approach to revealing yourself.
- *Ren and Stimpy* — Influential on humor and personality.
