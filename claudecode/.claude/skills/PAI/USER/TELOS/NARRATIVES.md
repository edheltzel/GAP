# Narratives

## The Imposter
Ed sees himself through the lens of imposter syndrome. Despite building PeptideWare, MyChron, Atlas, and a career in technology, he doesn't see himself as intelligent or as a real builder. Self-describes as "a fake liar." This internal narrative conflicts with external evidence but is deeply felt.

## The Tree (As Seen by Others)
Kristina sees Ed as the tree she leans on — stable, reliable, protective. Someone who would never do anything that doesn't benefit the family. Lucas sees dad as the person who gets it done no matter what — regardless of how tired, how injured, or how he feels. They see a strong leader, even though Ed doesn't see himself that way.

## Faith-Driven Family Unit
The family is a unit driven by God. Faith is the foundation. Self-talk and imposter syndrome are shared challenges within the family, but the core narrative is: we are strong together, guided by faith.
