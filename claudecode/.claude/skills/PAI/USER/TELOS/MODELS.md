# Mental Models

## Stay Between the Lanes
The truth lives in the middle, not in extremes. Stay between the lanes as much as possible because those lanes hold truth.

## Gut-Driven Decisions
Business decisions are usually based on a gut feeling. Not very systematic, but intuition has proven reliable over time.

## Need-Driven Building
Software gets built when there's a need that must be filled. The need drives the function.

## Double Duty Principle
When a customer project comes in, think through what can be reused to expand internal systems. Play double duty on the same dollars and the same time. DRY applied to business, not just code.
