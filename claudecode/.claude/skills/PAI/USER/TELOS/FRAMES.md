# Frames

## Family Protection Filter
Every decision gets filtered through "does this benefit and protect my family?" Ed has a 14-year-old son Lucas and wife Kristina (who has a progressive disease). Financial security and stability for them is the first lens applied to any opportunity or risk.

## Scenario Simulation
Runs scenarios in his head before acting. Systems thinking applied to decisions — mentally modeling outcomes before committing. Optimizes for: family benefit, quality of life improvement, and distinguishing gut instinct from emotional reaction.

## Read the Room
Before speaking or acting, assess the situation. Conservative approach — speak when spoken to. Observe first, act second. Don't reveal yourself until you understand the dynamics.

## Gut vs. Emotion Check
Distinguishes between genuine intuition (which he trusts) and emotional reactions (which he's wary of). A secondary filter that asks: "Is this a real signal or am I reacting?"
