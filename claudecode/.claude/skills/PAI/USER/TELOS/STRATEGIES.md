# Strategies

## Double Duty Business Model
Use client projects at PeptideWare to simultaneously expand internal systems. Same dollars, same time, double output.

## PeptideWare-to-MyChron Pipeline
Generate revenue through creative services and hosting to fund the long-term MyChron vision.

## AI-First Development
Leverage AI (Atlas/PAI) as a force multiplier for development, reducing dependence on hired expertise.

## Customer Service as Instinct
Customer service is core and non-negotiable. Willing to eat direct costs (e.g., reshipping duplicate orders at PeptideWare when originals go astray) to preserve it. This is a differentiator, not overhead — worth recognizing as a strategic asset, not just a cost of doing business.

## Shield Kristina From Money Stress
Carry the financial worry alone by design. Kristina grew up and lived much of her adult life with financial anxiety; Ed actively protects her from re-entering that headspace. She knows the general state, not the cash-flow-by-cash-flow weight. Boundary held deliberately.
