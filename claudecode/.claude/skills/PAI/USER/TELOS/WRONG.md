# Things I Was Wrong About

- That I'm always right. Humility comes with age over time.
- That I would never be that old person who can't move. But no, I am.
- That I could trust a 10-year friendship at face value. My BJJ professor, someone I considered my best friend for 10 years, turned out to be manipulative and a liar. He tried to drag me into drama that could have ruined my marriage and put my family and businesses at risk. In 10 years, he never once broke bread in my home. The only thing we had in common was Jiu-Jitsu. This connects to my own wisdom: the ones you think will always be there are the ones that go away first.
- That Donald Trump would be different from other politicians. Supported him the first time around based on his stated principles, but this second term he's doing exactly what every other politician has done. Not draining the swamp as claimed. Political trust and confidence in the US as a superpower is now uncertain.
- That I should never compromise on pricing. The last four low-budget clients all burned me. Gut instinct is reliable for big decisions, but client selection requires more deliberate criteria than instinct alone.
