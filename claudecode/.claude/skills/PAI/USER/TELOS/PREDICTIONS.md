# Predictions

## Technology
- AI advancement and new computing power (smaller PCBs, more powerful Arduinos and Raspberry Pis) will create more utility in embedded/micro computing
- People will stop hiring companies like PeptideWare for expert development skills, using AI to fill those gaps in their own abilities instead
- Personal operating systems will emerge — people building their own utilities and applications to serve their needs instead of conforming to someone else's system
- SaaS will decline; perpetual licensing will return. People will purchase and own software forever instead of subscribing

## AI Bubble (March 2026)
- The AI bubble is about to burst. Investment and utility will correct, though adoption is already there.
- AI will not replace engineers, developers, or designers — that's marketing hype, not reality.
- The general consumer doesn't understand that AI is programming/automation repackaged as a marketing trend.
- Ed's position: anti-hype, not anti-AI. Built Atlas and a business around AI while seeing the broader bubble clearly.

## Society
- More people will become dependent on government aid
- More war is coming, but it will be fought digitally
