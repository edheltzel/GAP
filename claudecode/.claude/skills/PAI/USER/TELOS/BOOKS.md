# Books

- *Building a Second Brain* by Tiago Forte — Knowledge management and personal information systems
- *The Bible* — Foundation of faith and family. The most influential book in Ed's life.
- *The Art of War* by Sun Tzu — Shaped business thinking and strategic approach.
- *The Art of the Deal* by Donald Trump — Business perspective (never completed cover to cover).
- *1984* by George Orwell — Reinforced distrust of surveillance and micromanagement. Imposed a thought process about privacy and control.
- Technical books (e.g., *How to Build a Website for Dummies* era) — The hands-on technical books that taught Ed to build and shaped how he thinks about problem-solving.
